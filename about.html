About The Long Run

Last updated Sept 2026

The Long Run is a plain-English guide to money for people who have better things to do than obsess over it — parents, first-time homebuyers, people paying down debt, and anyone trying to figure out retirement without a finance degree.

We focus on decisions that actually move the needle: building a real emergency fund, paying off debt in the right order, understanding what your employer's 401(k) match is worth, and knowing when renting beats buying (and vice versa).

What we don't do

We don't sell get-rich-quick advice, we don't push trading strategies, and we don't recommend anything we haven't looked into ourselves. If a page includes a link to a product or service, that's disclosed clearly.

Questions or corrections

If something on the site is unclear or out of date, we want to know — reach out any time through the contact page.
