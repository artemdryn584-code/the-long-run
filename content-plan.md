## Notes for writing
- Lead each article with the reader's actual situation, not a definition.
- Keep paragraphs short; this audience reads on mobile during downtime.
- Every article should end with one clear, doable next step.
- Disclose any affiliate links clearly, near the link itself, not just in the privacy policy.
