Family Guides

Teaching kids about money, splitting finances with a partner, life insurance, and other money questions that come up as a family.

Money decisions get more complicated once other people are involved — a partner with a different income, kids who are watching how you handle cash, an insurance policy that needs to actually match your family's situation instead of whatever a salesperson recommended. This section covers the financial questions that come up specifically because you're managing money as a household rather than as an individual: how to split expenses fairly when incomes differ instead of defaulting to an even 50/50 split, what level of life insurance coverage actually makes sense for parents, how to teach kids real financial habits without a complicated allowance system, and how to handle the predictable-but-irregular costs — open enrollment, back-to-school expenses, a sudden windfall — that hit a family budget differently than a solo one.
