Saving Guides

Building an emergency fund, deciding how much to save, and other practical ways to keep cash on hand without a spreadsheet obsession.

Saving well isn't about cutting every latte — it's about having the right amount of cash in the right place for the right reason. That means a real emergency fund sized to your actual expenses, not a round number that sounds responsible; a savings rate that adjusts to your income and debt instead of a flat 20% rule that ignores your situation; and separate sinking funds for the predictable-but-irregular costs — car repairs, holidays, annual insurance bills — that wreck an otherwise fine budget every time they show up "unexpectedly." This section also covers where to actually keep that money, since a high-yield savings account earning real interest beats a checking account earning nothing, and how state tax rules and budgeting apps fit into the bigger picture of keeping cash on hand without obsessing over it.
